Global Citizenship quiz

Grade 7 English: Term 1

Two practice tests based on the question styles in Year 7 reading papers and the Pearson-aligned Term 1 study checklist. The passages and questions are original. These are not official Pearson papers.

Choose a test

  • Test A: Reading, Grammar & Persuasive Writing — read a mystery and a food-waste appeal, then write a leaflet.
  • Test B: Reading, Grammar & Narrative Writing — read a story and an investigation report, then write a story ending.

Each test has 21 numbered questions worth 64 marks: 28 for reading, 12 for grammar and editing, and 24 for writing. Allow about 80 minutes per test, including proofreading.

Type your answers or work on paper. Use Submit & Print on either test to print your responses or choose Save as PDF. Nothing is sent or saved by the site.
